### 🔬 About Me

I am a biostatistician and public health scientist with a decade of experience applying statistical methods, artificial intelligence, and machine learning to real-world health problems. My doctoral research develops **multimodal deep learning frameworks** for predicting high-risk suicide, substance use disorders, and drug misuse by integrating clinical, behavioral, and population-level data.

### 🧠 Research Interests

- Artificial intelligence and deep learning for public health and clinical decision support
- Multimodal deep learning for behavioral and clinical outcomes prediction
- Cancer epidemiology, oncology clinical trials, and health disparities research
- Tobacco control and substance use research
- Multimorbidity and frailty in aging populations
- Bayesian methods and adaptive clinical trial design
- Health economics, outcomes research (HEOR), and regulatory science
